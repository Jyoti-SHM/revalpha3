How we fit into your stack

Your systems stay yours. We add the revenue layer.

Every hotel runs on a set of systems. RevAlpha sits alongside them and focuses on one job: making better revenue decisions and helping you act on them.

Your PMS

Your property management system stays your system of record for reservations, rooms and guests. RevAlpha works alongside it, not instead of it.

Your channel manager

Your channel manager keeps rates and inventory in sync across channels. Where access allows, we work within it to support the pricing and distribution actions in your plan.

OTA extranets

Listings, rates and content on each OTA are managed within the agreed access, as part of your wider pricing and distribution strategy.

RevAlpha

The revenue layer on top: strategy, dynamic pricing recommendations, forecasting, competitor intelligence, reporting and reviews.
