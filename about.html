About RevAlpha

A revenue department for hotels that never had one.

RevAlpha is an outsourced hotel revenue management team, built by Hospitality Minds. We exist for the independent hotels, resorts and groups that need professional revenue management without the cost and complexity of a full in-house department.

Who we are

Built by people who work with hotels every day.

RevAlpha brings together revenue strategy, daily pricing decisions, OTA management and reporting into a single team. Our parent company, Hospitality Minds, has worked with 800+ hotels across 48 cities in India, so our revenue thinking is grounded in what actually happens on the ground.

We are not a software company selling a dashboard. We are a team of revenue managers who watch your numbers, make recommendations and help you act on them.

What we do

Give every independent hotel a professional revenue function.

Large chains have revenue managers, forecasting tools and distribution teams. Most independent hotels do not. RevAlpha closes that gap with a service that fits the scale, budget and reality of an independent property. You get the strategy, the daily decisions and the reporting, without building a department from scratch.

How we work

Four principles that shape every engagement.

01

Revenue is a daily habit, not a monthly report

The hotels that earn more are the ones making small, informed decisions every day. We build that rhythm into your property so pricing is never left to chance.

02

We say what we can and cannot control

We do not guarantee occupancy, ADR or revenue growth. We provide strategy, execution and daily monitoring, and we are transparent about the limits of what any revenue team can promise.

03

Your data stays yours

We collect the access we need for the agreed scope and work within it. Access is used only for the revenue management activities in your plan.

04

Clarity beats jargon

Owners should understand their own revenue picture. We report in plain language, with clear actions, owners and deadlines.
