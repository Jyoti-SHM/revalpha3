Background

A quality property with limited online reach.

Star Palace Rameshwaram is a pilgrim and leisure hotel in Rameshwaram, Tamil Nadu. The property primarily serves religious travelers visiting Ramanathaswamy Temple, along with families and leisure guests.

Although the hotel had quality infrastructure and strong guest service standards, limited online visibility and absence from major OTA platforms restricted market reach and booking potential.

The transformation

The strategy focused on distribution, visibility, conversion and reputation, creating a stronger digital demand engine for the property.

The property was onboarded across major OTA platforms with optimized listings, room content, pricing and inventory management.

02

Keyword optimization, competitive pricing, enhanced room content, inventory synchronization and review monitoring were used to improve digital visibility and conversion opportunities.
